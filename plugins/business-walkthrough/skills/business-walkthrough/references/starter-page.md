# The starter page

Before the meeting, Adam's team writes one page about the business: what APG already believes,
from their website, public records and earlier conversations. Every line is a statement to
**check**, not a fact. It sits in this Project's knowledge, or someone drops it into the chat.

## How to use it

- **Read it before you say anything.** Then read the website it names, if there is one.
- **Turn every question it answers into a confirmation.** Don't ask "What do you make?" when the
  page lists the range. Ask "Your starter page lists these. Is that the whole range?" A
  confirmation doesn't count toward the 20 questions.
- **Play it back as "your starter page says…".** It is APG's understanding, not theirs, until the
  room says yes.
- **Put each "Where the sources don't agree" item to the room** in the part it belongs to (a
  product question in Part 1, a site question in Part 2). Whatever the room says goes on the page,
  marked `confirmed in the room`. If the room isn't sure either, it goes on the parking list.
- **Never read the Source lines aloud.** They're for APG.
- If there's no starter page, carry on without one and ask the questions in full. It will take
  longer, so tell Adam.

## The format APG writes

The page always has this heading, then these six sections, in this order. Every line ends with
`Source: …`.

```markdown
# [Business]: what we think we know so far

## What you make
- [A product line, or a fact about how it's made]. Source: [web address, or "our call with [role], [date]"]

## Who buys, and how work arrives
- …

## Sites and regions
- …

## Your teams
- …

## Tools and systems
- …

## Where the sources don't agree
- **[Topic].** [What one source says; what the other says. A question for the room.] Source: …
```

## Example (Ridgeline Steel, made up)

```markdown
# Ridgeline Steel: what we think we know so far

## What you make
- Stair stringers, balustrades, balustrade posts, handrail brackets and gates. Source: https://ridgelinesteel.example/products

## Who buys, and how work arrives
- Builders and shopfitters, mostly by emailed plans. Source: https://ridgelinesteel.example/contact

## Sites and regions
- Office and workshop in Newcastle; a yard in Brisbane. Source: https://ridgelinesteel.example/contact

## Your teams
- Sales, Drafting, Workshop and Accounts are named in job ads. Source: a public job board

## Tools and systems
- A CRM and a separate job system, from our first call. Source: our call with the Managing Director, 1 October 2026

## Where the sources don't agree
- **Gates.** The products page lists gates; the price list doesn't. Do you still make them? Source: https://ridgelinesteel.example/products
```
