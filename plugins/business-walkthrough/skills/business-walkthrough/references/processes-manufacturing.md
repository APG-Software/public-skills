# What each team looks after: a manufacturer

A typical list of what each team looks after in a manufacturing business that designs, makes and
delivers its own products. It is a starting point for a tick-list, **not a list of costs** and not
a measure of anyone.

**How it's used.** It is **not** read out in the walkthrough. After the meeting, Adam sends each
team lead the section for their team, through the person running the project on the business's
side, as a tick-list: tick what your team does, cross out what it doesn't, add what's missing. The
answers help plan the team sessions. Rename teams to match the business; drop the sections that
don't apply.

## Sales and estimating
- Answer new enquiries and log them
- Read plans and take off quantities
- Price and send quotes
- Follow up quotes
- Set up new trade accounts
- Hand a won job over to the next team

## Design and drawings
- Turn a won job into drawings
- Send drawings out for sign-off
- Manage changes and versions
- Release signed-off drawings to production

## Engineering
- Check designs and loads
- Certify what needs certifying
- Answer technical questions from customers before and after the sale
- Support new product development

## Procurement
- Order raw material and parts
- Manage imports and shipping
- Chase late deliveries
- Keep supplier details and prices up to date

## Production scheduling
- Turn sold jobs into work orders
- Sequence the shop floor
- Re-plan when material or drawings are late
- Release finished jobs to dispatch

## Manufacture
- Cut, form, weld or cast to the work order
- Record what's been made
- Report problems back to scheduling or design

## Quality
- Check finished work against the drawings
- Record and follow up faults
- Keep compliance records

## Stock and dispatch
- Keep track of stock in each yard
- Pick and pack orders
- Book trucks, drivers and cranes
- Deliver and collect proof of delivery

## Install
- Plan installs with the customer's site
- Send crews or arrange the installer
- Sign off finished installs

## Invoicing and accounts
- Raise invoices and progress claims
- Follow up payments
- Run credit checks for trade accounts
- Pay suppliers
- Month-end reporting

## People and culture
- Hire and induct new starters
- Keep training and licences current
- Payroll and leave
- Safety records

## In-house software
- Keep the business's own systems running
- Make changes the teams ask for
- Look after access and data
